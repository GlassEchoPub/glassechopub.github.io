Meet the author

B. Tomago

Gothic fiction. Supernatural love.
A soft spot for misunderstood creatures.

B. Tomago writes gothic fiction where haunted places, buried secrets, and supernatural love intertwine. Drawn to complicated characters and the uneasy space between fear and fascination, she explores grief, longing, and the connections that make us brave—or lead us somewhere we should never have gone.

Her novel, Blackwood, invites readers into a world of restless spirits, dangerous intimacy, and mysteries waiting beneath the floorboards.

When she isn’t writing, she can often be found sketching moonlit doorways, getting lost in music, or spending time with her family and an eclectic collection of much-loved animals. She has a soft spot for misunderstood creatures, in fiction and in life.
